Terrain Hole Tiles

Convert DayZ world positions into heightmap tile coordinates for a world's Holes config. Paste positions from the game or an editor, paint or erase tiles on the grid, then copy or download the finished config.

Convert a position

Tile — Type the X and Z from the game.

Pasting a whole position such as 5670.22, 276.72, 13661.87 into either box fills both; the height in the middle is ignored. Copy gives the tile as { x, z }, ready for a tiles[] list.

Positions

One per line. X, Y, Z as DayZ prints it (height in the middle is ignored), X Z pairs, or tiles typed directly as { 1417, 3415 }. Text after # is a label. Lines without numbers, such as class or tiles[] =, are skipped, so you can paste an existing tiles[] block back in.

Each map keeps its own positions and edits.

Click or drag to paint tiles; start on a tile that's in your list to erase instead. Scroll to zoom. To pan, use Move view, hold Shift, or drag with the right mouse button. Ctrl+Z undoes.

How the tiles work. A tile is one heightmap cell. Its index is the world coordinate divided by the terrain's grid size, rounded down, counted from the south-west corner: X first, then Z (north). Tile {x, z} covers x·g to (x+1)·g and z·g to (z+1)·g metres.

    
Nasdara status. The 4 m grid, the vanilla hole list and the config merge were measured on a dedicated server (1.30). A mod's Holes group was added alongside the vanilla groups, SurfaceIsHole reported exactly the new tiles as holes, and the hole has since been checked in game. Load the mod on clients too, since the hole has to exist in their terrain as well.
